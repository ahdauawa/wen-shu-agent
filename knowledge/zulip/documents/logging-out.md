## Log out of a Zulip account on one device

**Platform: Desktop/Web**

1. Click the **gear** () icon in the top
right corner of the app.
1. Click **Log out**.

**Platform: Mobile**

1. Tap the **menu** () tab in the bottom right corner of the app.

1. Tap the **switch account** () button.

1. Tap the **ellipsis** ()
to the right of the Zulip organization you want to log out of.
1. Tap **Log out**.
1. Approve by tapping **Log out**.

Logging out doesn't affect any other organizations you may be logged in
to. To log out of all your Zulip organizations, repeat the steps above
for each one.

## Log out of a Zulip account on all devices

Resetting the [API key](/api/api-keys) for a Zulip account will immediately log
you out of that account on all mobile devices. Resetting your password will log
you out of all browsers and desktop apps.

### Change your password and API key

1. Click the gear icon in the upper right of the web or desktop app.
1. Select **Personal settings**.
1. On the left, click **Account & privacy**.

1. Under **Account**, click **Change your password**.
1. Enter your old password and your new password, and click **Change**.
1. Under **API key**, click **Manage your API key**.
1. Enter your new password, and click **Get API key**.
1. Click **Generate new API key**.

If you've forgotten your password, you can [reset
it](/help/change-your-password#if-youve-forgotten-or-never-had-a-password)
using your email account.
